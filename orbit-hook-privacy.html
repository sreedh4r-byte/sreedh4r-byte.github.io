Privacy Policy — Orbit Hook

Effective Date: November 26, 2026

Orbit Hook (com.orbithook.game) respects your privacy.

Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Orbit Hook displays optional rewarded ads and periodic interstitial ads powered by Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may automatically collect and process limited device and ad interaction data in order to serve and measure ads (subject to your consent choices via Google's User Messaging Platform in supported regions such as the EEA/UK):

  • Device & Advertising Identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID)
  • Approximate Location (derived from IP address)
  • App Activity & Ad Diagnostics (ad impressions, clicks, crash logs, performance telemetry)

Learn how Google uses data from apps using AdMob here:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Developer Data Collection

  • The Orbit Hook developer does not operate external user accounts or backend servers and does not directly collect, store, or sell personal information such as your name, email address, contacts, or precise GPS location.

Local Storage

  • High scores, Daily Challenge dates/streaks, unlocked comet themes, and audio/haptics/sky preferences are stored locally on your device (user://save.cfg). Uninstalling the app deletes this local save data.

Permissions

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to load and display Google AdMob ads and consent forms.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely for optional tactile haptic feedback during gameplay and menu taps (toggleable in Pause settings).

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the developer via the support email listed on Orbit Hook's Google Play Store page.
